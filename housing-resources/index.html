A note from G

I attended a local housing resources fair and was reminded just how many programs exist to help people navigate housing challenges. Some can help with buying a home, while others can help people stay housed, make necessary repairs, find housing, or get through a housing emergency.

I put this page together as a starting point so you don't have to search for every resource yourself.

Eligibility, funding, income limits and program availability can change, so always confirm the current requirements directly with the organization providing the assistance.

G

Buying a Home

Down payment and closing cost help, homebuyer education, and programs for first-time and first-generation buyers. Assistance funds typically reopen each year and can run out quickly, so it helps to take a homebuyer class and talk with a lender early.

Woodbury homes Loan

Woodbury First-Time Homeownership Assistance

City of Woodbury, administered by the Washington County CDA

Low- and zero-interest down payment loans for first-time buyers purchasing a home in Woodbury. Lower rates may be available for veterans, adults over 65 and first-generation buyers.

Good to know: Start with your lender. Applications are due at least 35 days before closing.

  • 651-458-0936
  • WoodburyDPA@washingtoncountycda.org
Visit website for Woodbury First-Time Homeownership Assistance (opens in a new tab) ↗
Washington County homes Grant

First-Generation Homebuyer Grant

Washington County CDA

A $5,000 grant for first-generation homebuyers purchasing in Washington County. Income limits and a homebuyer education requirement apply.

Good to know: Funds are first-come, first-served and can run out; new funding typically opens each year.

  • 651-202-2820
  • firstgen@washingtoncountycda.org
Visit website for First-Generation Homebuyer Grant (opens in a new tab) ↗

Before you reach out

Information on this page is provided as a starting point and is not a guarantee of eligibility or assistance. Programs, funding, income limits, requirements and availability can change. Please contact the organization directly to confirm current requirements and availability.

This is a directory, not financial, legal, housing or lending advice. I don't run these programs or decide who qualifies. Last reviewed September 2026. If you notice something out of date, let me know and I'll update it.
